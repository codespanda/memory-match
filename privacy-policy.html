Memory Match Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Memory Match is a card-matching memory game for children, made by codespanda.com ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Memory Match app for Android phones, tablets and Android TV handles information.

The short version

Memory Match does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no chat.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, email address, photo, location or any other personal information, and it does not send information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers one setting, whether sound is on or off, on your device so that it stays the same next time. This setting never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising and third parties

The app shows no advertising and contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions that need your approval. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Links out of the app

The menu shows "A product by codespanda.com". Before that link opens the codespanda.com website in a browser, a grown-up must answer a question (a parental gate), so children cannot leave the app by themselves. Websites have their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Memory Match is designed for young children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. The app is designed to comply with Google Play's Families policies and with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and GDPR-K (European Union).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy: contact@codespanda.com or codespanda.com.
